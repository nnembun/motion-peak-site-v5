Common questions from plumbing businesses.

How does the AI receptionist work during an emergency? ▼
When someone books an emergency through your website, the AI instantly qualifies: "Is this a burst pipe, blocked drain, or boiler issue? What's your postcode? Can you confirm your address?" It then books the next available emergency slot and sends them a confirmation with your ETA. If you're fully booked, it gets their details and queues them for callback as soon as you're available. All of this happens in under 60 seconds.
Can the AI handle multiple languages? ▼
Yes. Many plumbing teams serve multi-lingual areas. The AI can be set up to detect the customer's language preference and communicate in English, Polish, Romanian, or other common languages in your service area.
What happens if there's no availability? ▼
The AI qualifies the emergency level. Critical emergencies (burst pipes, boiler down) get flagged for immediate callback. Non-urgent issues get queued and the customer receives automatic updates on when you can attend. They're never left wondering when you'll call back.
How do you handle jobs that aren't emergencies? ▼
The website handles both emergency and non-emergency work. Regular installations, maintenance, and quote requests use the same system but follow different workflows. Emergencies get priority; everything else flows into your standard sales process.
Can we use this with our existing tools? ▼
Yes. The AI system integrates with most scheduling software, CRM systems, and accounting tools. If you use a specific booking or job management system, we can connect it during setup.
What's the cost compared to hiring an admin? ▼
An AI system costs a fraction of a full-time receptionist (£250-500/month vs. £2,500+ salary) and works 24/7. You get better availability and consistency. Think of it as hiring a receptionist who never takes time off and handles multiple jobs simultaneously.
