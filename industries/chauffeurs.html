Why luxury transport businesses struggle online.

Your clients are busy professionals and corporate decision-makers. They don't have time for slow websites or unclear pricing.

1

Website Looks Like a Generic Taxi Service

Your website doesn't reflect the premium nature of your service. No differentiation from standard cab apps. High-end clients immediately click away to competitors who look more professional.

2

Pricing & Transparency Missing

Corporate clients need to know rates upfront. If your website doesn't show pricing clearly, they assume you're expensive and book someone else. Or they assume you're cheap and low-quality.

3

No Fleet Showcase

Photos of your vehicles sell the service. A well-maintained Mercedes S-Class or Range Rover tells a story. No photos = no proof of quality. Prospects don't book what they can't see.

4

Impossible to Book Online

Corporate PAs need to book quickly. If they have to call for a quote, email back and forth, and wait for confirmation, they'll use your competitor who has instant online booking.

5

No Corporate Profile

No insurance information, credentials, or corporate policies visible. Fortune 500 companies won't book without proof of insurance and proper company background. You lose six-figure contracts.

Questions from transport operators.

How do we handle corporate invoicing and billing? ▼
The booking system can automatically generate invoices with corporate reference numbers, cost codes, and billing details. Recurring corporate clients can have standing accounts with monthly invoicing. Payment can be on-booking or on-account depending on your relationship with the client.
Can we offer different rates to regular corporate clients? ▼
Yes. The system supports corporate account pricing, volume discounts, and loyalty rates. Regular clients see their negotiated pricing when they log in. You can offer competitive rates to large accounts while maintaining full pricing for one-off bookings.
How do we handle bookings from travel agencies? ▼
The website can have a separate corporate booking portal for travel agencies with their own login, pricing, and booking process. They integrate into your system via API so bookings flow directly to your dispatcher. No manual entry required.
What information do corporate clients need to see? ▼
Insurance details, licenses, safety certifications, driver backgrounds, emergency contact procedures, and complaint handling policies. The more transparent you are, the more comfortable Fortune 500 procurement teams feel approving you for their approved vendor list.
Can we track premium booking trends? ▼
Absolutely. We build analytics tracking which vehicle types sell best, peak booking times, average booking value, and client acquisition costs. You'll know which markets are most profitable and where to invest more marketing.
How do we handle last-minute bookings? ▼
The online system shows real-time availability. If a vehicle is available in 30 minutes, it's available to book. For urgent requests, clients can call for immediate confirmation. The website handles standard advance bookings while your team handles urgent requests over the phone.
