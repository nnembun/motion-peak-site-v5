Why electricians struggle to stand out.

The problem isn't getting local searches. The problem is converting those searches into jobs.

1

Competitors Have Slicker Websites

When someone compares you to 3-4 other electricians online, whoever has the better-looking website gets the click. A dated site loses jobs before you even get a chance to quote.

2

No Portfolio of Your Work

Text saying "commercial installations" means nothing. Photos of completed rewires, panels, and installations prove competence. Prospects want to see real work.

3

Quote Requests Pile Up

You get enquiries but can't respond fast enough while on-site. Someone else quotes first and wins the job. You never even get a chance to compete on price or experience.

4

Credentials Not Visible

Prospects don't know if you're Part P certified, insured, qualified. Without clear proof of credentials, some won't even contact you. Others shop price instead of quality.
