Questions about web design in Birmingham.

Do we need to meet in person? ▼
No. Everything happens remotely via video call, email, and WhatsApp. You stay in your office running your business while we build your website. Even though we're UK-based, working remotely keeps costs down and makes the process faster.
How long does a website take to build? ▼
A typical premium website takes 4-8 weeks from start to launch, depending on complexity. AI automation systems can be integrated into existing websites in 2-3 weeks. We work efficiently to get you live quickly without rushing quality.
What about support after launch? ▼
We provide ongoing support packages. Updates, security patches, performance monitoring, form management, and AI system updates. You can choose pay-as-you-go or a monthly support agreement depending on your needs and how hands-on you want to be.
Can you migrate my existing website? ▼
Yes. We can migrate content from your current website while redesigning it completely. We handle URL redirects and SEO preservation so you don't lose Google rankings in the process. Your existing authority transfers to the new site.
How much does a website cost? ▼
Websites typically start at £2,500 and go up depending on complexity and features. AI automation adds £250-500/month. Content packages are customizable. We work within your budget and always show ROI clearly. Schedule a consultation for a specific quote based on your needs and goals.
