Common questions about web design and AI systems.

How long does a premium website take? ▼
A typical premium website takes 4-8 weeks from discovery to launch, depending on complexity and how many revisions you want. We work efficiently without cutting corners. AI automation can be added to existing sites in 2-3 weeks.
How much does this cost? ▼
Premium websites typically start at £2,500-4,000 depending on features. AI automation adds £250-500/month. Content packages are customizable. We always show clear ROI. Book a free consultation for a custom quote based on your needs.
Do we need to meet in person? ▼
No. Everything happens remotely via video call, email, and WhatsApp. You stay focused on your business while we build. We're UK-based but work with clients nationwide and keep costs down by working remotely.
What if we already have a website? ▼
We can migrate your existing content while completely redesigning. We handle URL redirects and SEO preservation so you don't lose your current Google rankings. Your existing authority transfers to the new site.
What support do you provide after launch? ▼
We offer ongoing support packages including updates, security patches, performance monitoring, and AI system updates. You can choose pay-as-you-go support or a monthly retainer depending on your needs.
Can you help with SEO? ▼
Yes. Every website we build includes technical SEO, mobile optimization, and local optimization for your area. We also offer content-based SEO and strategy services to help you rank for high-value keywords.
How do I get started? ▼
Book a free consultation on our website. We'll discuss your business, goals, and recommend the right solution for you. No obligation, no sales pitch—just honest advice about what will work.
