# MOTION PEAK — The Voyage
## Journey Map & Build Plan (v1 — for approval)

**Concept in one line:** The Motion Peak portfolio is not a website with a showreel — it IS the showreel. One continuous cinematic voyage through four visual worlds, each world secretly a portfolio piece, ending in the reveal that one studio built everything you just travelled through.

**Working headline:** "Design that stops the scroll." (fits brand voice; alternatives: "Websites built to be experienced." / "Digital Stories That Work While You Sleep.")

**Signature motion language (per brand.md):** the cinematic depth transition. Every scene change passes THROUGH something — clouds, a doorway, restaurant doors, a watch crystal. Never a fade-up. One motion language, repeated.

---

## THE JOURNEY — scene by scene

### Scene 0 — The Title Card (preloader)
- Black screen, MOTION PEAK wordmark, the orange play icon fills like a film-leader countdown as assets load.
- Copy: `MOTION PEAK — Digital design built to hold attention.`
- Purpose: sets the cinema frame; buys asset loading time.

### Scene 1 — The Descent (hero)
- **Visual:** scroll-scrubbed video frame sequence (canvas). Camera above the clouds at golden hour → dives through cloud layer → open ocean → a white cruise ship revealed below, tiny then huge.
- **Scroll mechanic:** pinned section; scroll drives the dive frame-by-frame. Headline parallaxes up and shreds into letters as you commit to the dive.
- **Copy:** `MOTION PEAK` / `Design that stops the scroll.` / service list line: `Website Design · AI Video · Flyer Design · Campaign Visuals` / CTA: `View the Portfolio`
- **Wow effect:** the drone dive itself + mouse-move parallax on cloud layers before scroll starts.

### Scene 2 — The Ship (selected work corridor)
- **Visual:** deck approach → pass through the ship's doors into a dark luxury corridor (this is the depth transition: the doorway mask swallows the viewport).
- **Scroll mechanic:** the corridor is a **horizontal scroll** section. Framed "windows" along the corridor = selected work, shown large: Nocara Dark, Watermelon Smoothie, Ebuka Nwokeji, concept pieces. Each frame tilts subtly on hover (mouse parallax), orange label on active.
- **Copy:** `Every project is a world.` / per-project: name, sector, service, `View Live Website` where real, `Studio Concept` label where not.
- **Purpose:** brand.md "Selected Work" — the portfolio surfaces 30 seconds in, shown huge.

### Scene 3 — The Docking (beach + Watermelon Smoothie feature)
- **Visual:** back on deck; scroll-scrubbed docking sequence — ship glides toward a white-sand beach, gangway meets the sand. On the beach: a minimal beach bar, a sealed glass bottle of watermelon juice sweating in the sun.
- **Scroll mechanic:** horizon-line wipe into beach world (palette shifts warm). Bottle assembles from pouring liquid as you scroll.
- **Copy:** `First stop: a brand we bottled.` / `WATERMELON — Summer, sealed in glass.` / `View Live Website →` (links to watermelon-smoothie.vercel.app)
- **Wow effect:** hover on the bottle = condensation drips respond to cursor; slight glass refraction.
- **Purpose:** live project #1 woven into the story as a destination, not a thumbnail.

### Scene 4 — The Restaurant (fine dining + exploding dish + Nocara Dark dessert)
- **Visual:** dusk walk from the beach to a restaurant. Two heavy closed doors fill the screen.
- **Scroll mechanic 1 — the doors:** scroll pushes the doors open (mask reveal) into a candlelit dining room.
- **Scroll mechanic 2 — the dish:** the signature dish EXPLODES into its ingredients — suspended mid-air, scroll scrubs the explosion out and back into a plated dish. (Birria-chi energy, elevated.)
- **Scroll mechanic 3 — the menu:** the menu presents as a full-screen typographic sequence, editorial serif, dishes revealed line by line. Dessert course: `Single-Origin Dark Chocolate` → the room darkens, gold light — `NOCARA DARK — Dark. Pure. Obsessive.` / `View Live Website →` (live project #2, as the dessert).
- **Copy:** `Fine dining, designed.` / `Studio Concept` label on the restaurant itself.
- **Purpose:** concept project (fine dining) + live project #2, chained in one scene.

### Scene 5 — The Watch (luxury timepiece world)
- **Visual:** camera drifts across the dining room to a diner's wrist as he checks the time. Macro zoom INTO the watch crystal — the crystal becomes the portal (depth transition) — inside: a black chrome world of floating timepieces.
- **Scroll mechanic:** exploded view of the movement — gears, springs, dial suspended in space, scroll rotates the constellation and reassembles the watch (deliberate echo of the food explosion: same motion language). Then a short horizontal run of three watch faces.
- **Wow effect:** watch hands track the cursor; on idle they show the real time.
- **Copy:** `Time to show you what this was.` / `Studio Concept — Timepiece Campaign`
- **Purpose:** concept project #2; the pivot line into the reveal.

### Scene 6 — The Reveal (services + flyers + AI video)
- **Visual:** pull OUT of the watch crystal — and out of a screen. The camera retreats to show a dark gallery: every world just visited hangs as a glowing framed screen. Wide shot: the Motion Peak studio.
- **Copy:** `You just scrolled through our portfolio.` / `One studio. Every world.` Then the four pillars, editorial columns: Website Design · AI Video Production · Flyer Design · Digital Campaign Visuals.
- **Sub-sections in the gallery:**
  - **AI Video wall** — large muted autoplay previews (only active one plays), short labels.
  - **Flyer room** — posters in chrome frames, layered stacks, close paper texture on hover.
- **Purpose:** brand.md services + AI video + flyer sections, still inside the fiction.

### Scene 7 — The Process (5 stages)
- Minimal editorial strip: Direction → Visual System → Production → Refinement → Delivery. One line each. Number counters tick as they enter.

### Scene 8 — The Invitation (contact)
- **Visual:** back above the clouds — where we started — but now the clouds part around a new, unbuilt horizon.
- **Copy:** `Your brand could be the next world.` / primary CTA `Message Motion Peak` / secondary `Start a Project` / footer: minimal nav (Work · Services · Studio · Process · Contact), logo, orange play icon.

## TECH APPROACH
- Vite + vanilla TS, GSAP ScrollTrigger (pinning, scrubbing), Lenis smooth scroll.
- Canvas frame-sequence player (preload ahead of playhead; poster frames; lazy per scene).
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: static hero frames + gentle opacity/position reveals, no scrub.
- Mobile: shorter sequences, vertical crops, simplified corridor (swipe cards), all content preserved.
- Accessibility: keyboard nav, focus states, alt text, contrast per brand palette.
- Local dev server only. **No deployment until explicitly approved.**
